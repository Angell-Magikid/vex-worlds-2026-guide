⚠️ Important Rules 重要规定

🚫

Student Centered Policy

Parents/coaches may not operate robots or give real-time instructions during matches.

家长/教练不得操作机器人或实时指挥比赛

⚠️

One Adult per Team in Practice Area

Only one adult per team allowed in the practice field area.

练习场每队仅限1位家长陪同

📋

Wristband & Badge Required

Must wear wristband and/or name badge at all times to enter Exhibition Hall and Dome.

全程佩戴手环和姓名牌，方可进入展厅和 Dome

🔧

Rubber Band Rule <R16>

Legal non-VEX rubber bands: ≤ 7.5" long, ≤ 0.25" wide.

非 VEX 橡皮筋：长≤7.5英寸，宽≤0.25英寸

🚫 严禁使用非官方橡皮筋

⚠️ 结论：不要使用非官方橡皮筋 / Do NOT use non-official rubber bands

📓 Bring Your Physical Notebook! 强烈建议携带实体 Notebook

📒

强烈建议携带 NOTEBOOK 实体

携带一本实物 Notebook，在 Inspection（检录）、Judge's Interview（裁判面试）环节可能需要用到，赛队也可以决定是否自愿分享给其他赛队。

✅ Inspection 检录 🎤 Judge's Interview 裁判面试 🤝 自愿分享给其他赛队

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Official Parent & Spectator Guidelines 官方家长观赛规范

🚫

<G1> Treat Everyone with Respect · 尊重每一个人

Behavior which will NOT be tolerated · 以下行为绝不容忍：

  • • Rude behavior · 粗鲁行为
  • • Match throwing · 故意输掉比赛
  • • Intentionally playing beneath ability to manipulate match results · 故意放水/意图干扰以操纵比赛结果
  • • Political statements/protests of any kind · 任何形式的政治声明或抗议
🎓

<G2> Student Centered Program · 以学生为中心

Behavior which will NOT be tolerated · 以下行为绝不容忍：

  • • Adult dictating match strategy, even in practice · 家长指挥比赛策略（包括练习）
  • • Sideline coaching · 场边指导
  • • Adults coding or building robots · 家长编程或搭建机器人
  • • Adults participating in post-match discussions · 家长参与赛后讨论
  • • Adults at robot inspection or in queueing · 家长在机器人检录或排队区域
  • • Adults at practice or skills fields · 家长进入练习场或技能赛场地

Source: REC Foundation Official Guidelines · 来源：REC Foundation 官方规范
