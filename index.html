⚠️ Important Rules 重要规定

🚫

Student Centered Policy

Parents/coaches may not operate robots or give real-time instructions during matches.

家长/教练不得操作机器人或实时指挥比赛

⚠️

One Adult per Team in Practice Area

Only one adult per team allowed in the practice field area.

练习场每队仅限1位家长陪同

📋

Wristband & Badge Required

Must wear wristband and/or name badge at all times to enter Exhibition Hall and Dome.

全程佩戴手环和姓名牌，方可进入展厅和 Dome

🔧

Rubber Band Rule <R16>

Legal non-VEX rubber bands: ≤ 7.5" long, ≤ 0.25" wide.

非 VEX 橡皮筋：长≤7.5英寸，宽≤0.25英寸
