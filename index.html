📜 Student Centered Policy 学生主导条款

VEX competitions are student-led and student-operated:

VEX 机器人比赛强调学生主导：

  • Students operate and program the robot independently
    学生独立操作和编程机器人
  • Adults may coach but cannot substitute or directly operate
    成年人仅可指导，不得代替操作
  • Students must complete all competition tasks on their own
    比赛过程中学生须独立完成所有任务
